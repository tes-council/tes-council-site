For 2023 we have had significant changes and developments within the council, including the departure of longstanding members and the introduction of new ones. The council aims to enhance stakeholder engagement and has received positive feedback on its initiatives.

Key activities included presentations at various conferences and plans for a new TES Council website to improve communication. Six council meetings were held, alongside numerous working group sessions focusing on three main areas: the TES Capability Roadmap, the maturation of PAS 280 standards, and the development of a skills and training strategy.

Looking ahead to 2024, the council aims to address operational enhancements, update standards, establish a Capability Network, launch a TES Skills strategy, and further elevate the profile of TES through increased stakeholder engagement.

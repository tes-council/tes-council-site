Why are increasing numbers of equipment and product manufacturers turning to the provision of through-life support services to enhance their market share and to access additional revenue and profit streams? Why are increasing numbers of equipment and product users buying these services, and more importantly re-buying them again and again?

The answer is that, in the right context, through-life support services add significant value to both parties by eliminating previously normalised waste in the buy, use, maintain and dispose life cycle of equipment and product ‘Assets’, and by placing the risk of through-life support decisions where it can best be managed.
